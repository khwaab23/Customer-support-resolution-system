# Returns & Eligibility

Policy ID: NTE-POL-01 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-10-01

## Purpose

Define when a NovaTech Electronics purchase can be returned. Applies only to fictional US store sales denominated in USD.

## Standard window

A customer may request a standard return within 30 calendar days after the recorded delivery date. A delivered date must be verified from the order/shipping system. Missing delivery evidence requires investigation rather than assumed eligibility.

## Required order facts

Verify authenticated customer ownership, order status, product and order-item ID, delivery date, previous returns, and requested return quantity.

## Receiving and inspection

A return can be requested and shipped before it is received. Refund eligibility requires that the return has physically arrived and inspection status equals PASSED.

## Inspection failures

An inspection marked FAILED does not qualify for an automatic refund proposal. Route the case to human review with the inspection reason and evidence.

## Late returns and exceptions

Requests outside 30 days are not automatically eligible. A reviewer may consider exceptions, but the LLM and regular support workflow must not override the policy.

## Partial or missing data

If item, delivery or return records are missing or contradictory, gather missing facts or escalate. Do not infer that a return passed inspection.

## Examples

RET-501 / ORD-1001: received 2026-10-14 and inspection PASSED; may proceed to refund evaluation. ORD-1003: return in transit; cannot yet propose issuance.
