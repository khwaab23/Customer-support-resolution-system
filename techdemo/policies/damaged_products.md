# Damaged Products

Policy ID: NTE-POL-04 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-10-01

## Purpose

Handle reported damaged products while collecting evidence and avoiding unsupported refunds or replacements.

## Initial verification

Require authenticated order ownership and confirm the relevant purchased item and delivery. Ask for the damage description and evidence where feasible.

## Evidence

Collect the affected item, a concise description, and optional photo metadata or mock attachment reference. Store only synthetic evidence in this MVP.

## Assessment

The agent may summarize the case and cite return/damage policies. It must not pretend to inspect images without an actual image-analysis step.

## Resolution

Prepare a review request or a suggested return workflow based on verified facts. Any eventual refund must follow the separate refund policy and human approval gate.

## Escalation

Escalate when ownership cannot be verified, evidence conflicts, policy applicability is unclear, or an exception is requested.

## Customer update

State what is known and which evidence or next action is needed. Never promise a replacement shipment; replacement service is outside the initial project scope.
